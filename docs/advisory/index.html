Engagement types

Three ways this usually starts

Scoped work with founders and investors. Fixed scope, written output, no retainer required.

  • Technical diligence

    A read on whether the architecture, team, and delivery model behind a round hold up — written for investors, usable by the founders.

    • Written findings: risks, severity, and what to do about each
    • Architecture and scalability read against the stated plan
    • Team and delivery-model assessment, not just code

  • Architecture de-risking

    For teams heading into a regulated launch or a scale step: find the parts that will break, and decide what to change before they do.

    • Target architecture with the trade-offs written down
    • Compliance and auditability designed in, not bolted on
    • Integration strategy for brokers, banks, and market data

  • Fractional CTO

    Ongoing technical partnership at a few days a month — roadmap, hiring bar, and the technical side of investor conversations.

    • Roadmap and sequencing you can defend to a board
    • Hiring bar, interview loop, and first senior hires
    • Support through diligence and investor questions
