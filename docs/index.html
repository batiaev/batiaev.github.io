Building

Two products of my own

Fintecy pulls a scattered financial life into one place. SHIP gives engineering teams a typed graph their agents can actually write to. Both are registered companies in private beta, and both started as problems I hit myself.

Fintecy

A single entry point for your financial life

Connect brokers and banks read-only, get FIFO realised P&L across accounts, and plan long-horizon with data you own.

Registered company with its own trademark and ICO data-protection registration — built to hold real account data, not just demo it.

Private beta · waitlist open

SHIP

A typed knowledge graph for engineering teams

Goals, decisions, and artefacts as typed nodes instead of prose. Agents read and write the graph over MCP, and every agent write carries provenance and an audit trail.

Provenance is the design constraint: each node records which agent or human wrote it, from which source, and what it superseded.

Private beta · waitlist open
